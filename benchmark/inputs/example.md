## Why sleep matters

- Memory consolidation — short-term to long-term during NREM/REM cycles.
- Immunity — one night under 6 hours can drop NK-cell activity ~70%.
- Mood — REM recalibrates the amygdala; less sleep, sharper reactivity.
- Metabolism — chronic short sleep raises insulin resistance and hunger hormones.

## A surprising stat

1 in 3 U.S. adults routinely get less than the recommended 7 hours per night.
Sleep debt is real — and unlike financial debt, you can't refinance it on the weekend.

## The four stages

- N1 — light, transitional, ~5% of the night.
- N2 — heart rate drops, sleep spindles, ~45%.
- N3 — deep slow-wave sleep, tissue repair, ~25%.
- REM — dream sleep, memory + emotion processing, ~25%.

## Myth vs reality

- Myth: you can train yourself to need less sleep. Reality: it's mostly genetic.
- Myth: older adults need much less sleep. Reality: similar need; quality declines.
- Myth: snoring is harmless. Reality: often a sign of sleep apnea.
- Myth: alcohol helps you sleep. Reality: it sedates and fragments REM.

## Habits that work

1. Anchor a consistent wake time, including weekends.
2. Get 10-20 minutes of morning daylight.
3. Keep the room cool (65-68°F).
4. Cut caffeine 8-10 hours before bed.
5. 30-60 minute wind-down with low light + low stimulation.

## Closing

Sleep is not the absence of doing — it's the work the brain does when you finally get out of its way.
